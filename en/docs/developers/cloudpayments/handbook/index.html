For recurring payments, you need to set restrictions on sending repeat requests if error codes from the "Unconditionally negative" group were received. This means that subsequent recurring payments will not go through, and you will need to perform the setup transaction again.


Unconditionally negative
5005 Do Not Honor
5057 Transaction Not Permitted
5003 Invalid Merchant
5017 Customer Cancellation
5033 Expired Card Pickup
5034 Suspected Fraud
5001 Refer To Card Issuer
5012 Invalid Transaction


After 2 attempts from the "Conditionally negative" and "Temporarily negative" groups, payment processing will be blocked for 24 hours. Keep this rule in mind on your side for the error codes below.
